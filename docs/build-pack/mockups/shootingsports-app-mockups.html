Decisions

QuestionDecisionWhy
Sign in or Log in“Sign in” on every entry point, app and website. “Log out” stays as the exit.The framework flagged the split. One verb for the way in reads as one product.
PaletteUse the live shootingsports.co.za colours: forest #0E3328, lime #C5E07A, ink #10251F, page #F7F8F5, link green #245D3F.The rendered site already moved off the gunmetal, olive and gold tokens the framework lists. Those variables are still in the stylesheet but are overridden. Updating the doc settles the web-versus-mobile palette question.
Unavailable directoriesUse the framework copy, “Clubs search opens here when its API endpoint lands,” and never a zero-results message or sample results.An empty directory reads as “nobody is listed,” which is false and hurts clubs.
Mockup dataSquare-bracket placeholders and N or M for counts. No sample club names, distances or day counts.Invented names in review mockups get copied into seed data and screenshots, and real venues end up implied as partners.
Future statesEvery future concept carries a visible tag and ships behind a feature flag.Reviewers should never mistake a concept for shipped functionality.
Status bar and device chromeNot drawn, but space is reserved for it.A fake clock and battery add noise and go out of date. The OS draws them.
iPhone safe areas59pt top inset above every header, 34pt home-indicator inset under the tab bar (49pt bar plus 34pt).Without it, titles sit under the Dynamic Island and tabs sit on the home indicator.
Back navigationTab roots have no back button. Every pushed screen (match detail, sport landing) has a back button labelled with its parent plus edge swipe. Sign in is a modal with a close button. Website links open in an in-app browser with Done.iPhones have no hardware or system back button, so the app must always provide one off a tab root.
Find to Calendar deep linkSwitches tab, no back button. The removable province chip is the way out.A tab switch is not a push. A back button there would break the iOS model.

Open questions

  1. Me section order for stacked roles. The framework puts Manage above saved matches. With four capabilities that pushes saved matches about a screen and a half down (see M5f). Option: keep Manage first for one capability, and collapse to a compact capability strip once there are three or more.
  2. Capability card spec. The paste cut off before it. Card items and action labels are placeholders until it’s confirmed what each card lists.
  3. Pro. Price, which Free limits Pro lifts, and the copy for the upsell.
  4. Trial end. What the pill and Me tab show on the last day and after expiry.
  5. Error colour. The site defines none. These mockups use #B3261E on a #FBEAEA tint, which passes contrast on the light page.
  6. Desk hand-off. Whether “Open club desk” in the app opens /desk in the browser or a scoped Filament panel per capability.

Build order

3. Role stackingManage cards from the shared capability payload, and the desk hand-offUsers with no capabilities and users with four are equally easy to read.

5. IncrementsPersonalised Home, follows, saved searches, directories, activity history, sport landing pagesEach lands with its own endpoint, not a stubbed list.

Definition of done

  • Every request shows loading and offers a way to recover from failure.
  • Every empty state says whether there is no data or the feature isn’t available yet.
  • App and website show the same plan and capability logic.
  • A four-capability user can reach saved matches and Log out without hunting.
  • Text meets 4.5:1 contrast, touch targets are at least 44px, focus is visible, and icon buttons have labels.
